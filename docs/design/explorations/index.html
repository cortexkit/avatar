Creature redesign — exploration grids (design note 008)

Look-first exploration for the v2 creature design. Each grid answered one question before the contract freezes. Regenerable: git archive <sha> playground | tar -x && cargo run; the SVGs here are the committed outputs of each grid's final state.

Grid 1 — body proportions × top parts

Question: does blob-space alone produce species-level characters, and which top parts make silhouettes? Answer: yes — mochi/ghost/troll/alien/robot from four floats; ears and antennae strongest.

Grid 2 — the life layer

Question: what makes them alive? Answer: blush + belly patch (always-on now), face packs for personality range (deadpan/gremlin/imp hold the ugly-cute end). Body wonk too subtle to matter — asymmetry budget moved to parts/face.

Grid 3 — palette recipes × cast + 28px strip

Question: procedural color harmony without the lookup table, and does identity survive icon size? Answer: four recipe bands keep (pastel, toy, dark-gravitas, earthy), two drop; the 28px strip passes decisively.

Grid 4 — mood freeze-gate (7 moods × cast)

Question: can the wire's 7-mood contract express on every face archetype? Answer: yes, including deadpan half-lids — lids/brows/mouth/squint/adjunct layers, mood provably orthogonal to identity. Found and fixed the brow mirror bug.

Grid 5 — the gacha test (36 random genomes)

Question: does "no ugly genome" hold on RANDOM input? Answer: 36/36 presentable from sequential seeds, dark band rare and striking, two near-collisions in 36 (fine at fleet scale).

Grid 6 — hue A/B (same genomes, golden-angle spacing)

Question: is hue clustering worth a fleet-wide allocator? Answer (recommendation): no — evenness gain is modest, silhouette+value already carry identity; ship stateless uniform hue, allocator addable later via the era store.
